Performance

Always on. Barely there.

Headroom is a native Swift app, built to sit in your menu bar all day without you ever noticing it. It sleeps when your Mac does, wakes only when there's something new to read, and draws every pixel itself, so it never pulls in heavyweight system graphics.

We drew our own glass.

The system's glass effects map a graphics archive of about 190 MB into any app that uses them. So Headroom paints its own surfaces on the CPU into small bitmaps, and keeps the open popover to about a sixth of the memory.

Sleeps when your Mac does.

It reads your limits every 5 minutes while you're working, every 15 when you're not, and not at all while your Mac is asleep or locked.

Listens instead of polling.

File-system events tell it when Claude Code or Codex write new usage, so it doesn't rescan your logs on a timer.

One timer, with slack.

A single timer with built-in tolerance lets macOS batch Headroom's wakeups with everything else's, to save energy.

Heavy lifting, then gone.

The first full scan of your history runs in a short-lived helper that exits when it's done, handing its memory straight back.

Native, through and through.

Swift and AppKit. No web views, no Electron, no runtime to drag along. Just a small app that does one job.

Its ledger, memory-mapped.

The record of what it has already counted lives in a mapped file, so its clean pages don't count toward Headroom's memory.

Setup

Ready in a minute.

A five-step welcome sets it up the first time. Every choice previews live and applies at once, and it's all in Settings later, each section beside a preview of what it changes.

  1. Pick your tools.Claude Code, Codex, or both.
  2. Shape the menu bar.Both tools or the most urgent, the week or the session.
  3. Tune the 5-hour warning.Warn at 50, 60, 75 or 90%.
  4. Choose notifications.Nearly used, limit hit, and back again.

Privacy

It only reads.

Headroom never sends a prompt, never uses one of your reset credits, and never changes your sign-in. There's no Headroom server and no sign-up. No analytics, ever.

Stays on your Mac.

Token counts come from files already on your Mac. Nothing you type is stored, and nothing is uploaded.

A short list.

Anthropic's usage page, your iCloud when sync is on, and, if you leave it on, the Claude and OpenAI status pages. That's all of it.

Your iCloud, encrypted.

Sync to iPhone and Watch goes through your own private iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. Prompts, projects and sign-ins never sync.

Local notifications.

Alerts are scheduled on your device. Nothing runs as root unless you turn on Fan Control.
