Headroom brings your Claude and Codex limits from your Mac to your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and answers one question: do you have room to spare this week, are you on track, or should you slow down?

THE RESERVOIR
One level, filled to what's left this week, with when it renews right beside it. Under it, the week's graph and each 5-hour session as a bar on the same time axis, so a run of near-misses stands out.

THE LIMIT YOU HIT FIRST
The 5-hour session gets a warning from 75% (or the level you set on your Mac), a Live Activity that counts down to the reset, and a note when it will run out before then. At the limit, it tells you exactly when you're back.

YOUR AGENT RUNS, AWAY FROM THE DESK
Follow your Claude Code and Codex runs from every Mac: which one needs you, how far along each is, and when it should finish. Get told when a run needs your input, stops with work left, or finishes.

ON YOUR WRIST AND HOME SCREEN
Complications, a watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size.

SEE WHERE THE TOKENS GO
Activity by hour and by model, what your usage is worth at API prices, and every past week.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Readings travel from your Mac through your own private iCloud, end-to-end encrypted. There is no Headroom server and no sign-up. Prompts, project names and file paths never leave your Mac.

Requires Headroom on a Mac with Claude Code or Codex signed in to a subscription, and iCloud sync turned on there.
