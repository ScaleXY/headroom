# iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch App Store assets

Everything here is generated by `make mobile-appstore`:

1. `Scripts/mobile_appstore_capture.py` drives the apps with UI tests (`Mobile/UITests`, project
   `Mobile/screenshots.yml`) in the iOS 27, iPhone Duo (iOS 27.1) and watchOS 27 simulators, on
   the apps' built-in sample data, so nothing shows live account data. Raw captures go to
   `build/appstore-mobile/raw/`.
2. `Scripts/mobile_appstore_media.py` builds the folders below from them.

Each simulator boots in a clock zone where it's 9:41 as capturing starts, so status bars read 9:41
to 9:43 with full signal, and the app and the Live Activity agree on the time. (The iPhone Lock
Screen clock settles on another zone a moment after waking, so that one shot reads 9:48.) The watch
flow takes longer, so the watch shots read 9:41 to 9:52.

## Two sets: pick one per device

| Folder | What |
| --- | --- |
| `1-normal/` | Screenshots exactly as each device draws them, and app previews as recorded (trimmed and encoded to the accepted size). |
| `2-captioned/` | The same screens in a device frame on one uniform blue (`#2F6DF6`, `BG` in the script) under a headline, at the same App Store sizes. |

Don't mix the two sets within one device size.

## Creative assets: product page header and search results

They appear on iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 and later (Product Page → Header and Search Results, or Asset
Library). Optional.

| File | Placement | Spec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `creative/header-3840x1646.png` | Product page header | 21:9 image |
| `creative/header-3840x1646.mp4` | Product page header | 21:9 video, 25.5 s, 30 fps, loops seamlessly |
| `creative/search-results-3840x2560.png` | Search results | 3:2 image, the largest accepted size |
| `creative/search-results-1920x1280.mp4` | Search results | 3:2 video, 29 s, 30 fps, loops seamlessly |
| `creative/universal-5244x2950.png` | Either placement | The 16:9 universal PNG. Pick **Use header asset in search results** to use it for both. |

Key copy stays inside the art safe areas in Apple's template (header: 1646 × 661, centered;
search results: 2168 × 1030, centered; universal: 1402 × 962 at 1921, 660). The devices outside
those areas may be cropped on some screens; check them with App Store Connect's product page preview.

## Worth knowing

- **Sample data.** The UI tests launch the app with `-HeadroomScreenshots YES`, which hides the
  "Sample data" note, and `-HeadroomDemoScenario`, which picks the sample accounts (`twoMacs` by
  default; `twoClaudeAccounts` for a third account; `showcase` adds a Codex work account paused at
  its 5-hour limit, for the watch). Without these arguments the app behaves as before.
- **iPhone Duo.** These are outer-display (folded) shots. The Duo simulator boots folded and can't
  be unfolded from a script. For inner-display shots (2007 × 2853, the split view), unfold it in
  DeviceHub, then capture that display by hand or adapt the `duo` entry in the capture script.
- **Live Activity countdown.** On the iOS 27 Lock Screen the system shows the countdown's seconds
  as "--" while the phone is locked (for example "47:--"). That's iOS, not the capture.
- **Brand marks.** The screens show the Claude and OpenAI marks as the app draws them. That's
  still an open question in the plans; keep it in mind for App Review.
- **Regenerating.** `make mobile-appstore` re-captures and rebuilds everything (about 35 minutes;
  it reuses the "Headroom Capture" simulators it made). `python3 -I Scripts/mobile_appstore_media.py` alone rebuilds the sets from
  the last raw captures, for example after editing a headline or `BG`.
