**Description** (4,000):

Headroom sits in your menu bar and answers one question about your Claude and Codex plans: do you have room to spare this week, are you on pace, or should you slow down?

PACE, NOT JUST A PERCENTAGE
82% left sounds great until you notice it renews tomorrow. Headroom compares what's left with the time left, draws your week so far, and tells you in one sentence whether to use more, keep going or ease off, and roughly when you'd run out.

YOUR WEEK AT A GLANCE
Each tool gets five cells in the menu bar. Blue means room to spare, white means on pace, amber and rose mean slow down. An hourglass shows when unused allowance is about to renew, and a countdown appears when your 5-hour session is nearly used.

THE 5-HOUR SESSION, FRONT AND CENTER
The 5-hour session is usually the limit you hit first, so it gets its own panel: five cells with even pace marked, a graph of the session so far and where it's heading, and a forecast from your recent pace. Past your warning level (75% by default) it turns amber, then rose. At the limit it tells you exactly when you're back.

SEE WHERE THE TOKENS GO
The Activity tab shows tokens and cost by hour and by model, every past week, and your recent 5-hour sessions, so a run of near-misses stands out.

NO SURPRISES
Headroom flags when a single model's weekly cap is the one to watch, notices early resets, and highlights reset credits before they lapse.

MADE TO FIT
• A five-step welcome with a live preview at every step
• Settings that preview each change before you leave
• Optional notifications when a session is nearly used, hits the limit, and comes back
• Light, dark or system appearance

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Headroom reads usage from the Claude Code and Codex apps you're already signed in to. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Requires Claude Code or Codex signed in to a subscription.
